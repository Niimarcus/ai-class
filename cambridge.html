Privacy rule: check before you paste into AI

Remove first:
  • Street address
  • Phone number
  • ID, visa, A-number or case numbers
  • Date of birth
  • Other people's names, phones and emails
OK to keep:
  • Your name, city and email
  • Your jobs, dates and schools
  • Your skills and numbers

Your public page: references say "Contact on request". No line about work authorization.

Your questions. In class you use the Sparecycle class chat. It is free. You do not need an AI account. You get 20 questions each time you join. Each class needs about 1 to 6. For the teacher: projector script (Class 1)

How the class works: I do, we do, you do.

  • Together steps (mechanical clicks): you do the step on the projector. Students do the same step. Then go to the next step.
  • On your own steps: each person's work is different. Do not wait for the room. Walk around.
  • Checkpoint check at the end of each checkpoint: "Show your card: green = I see X, red = I need help." Helpers go to the red cards. You keep going.
  • Show the step number on the screen before each step, for example "Step 2.1". Put this page next to your demo window, or write the number on the whiteboard. Students who fall behind find their place with it.

Before class

  1. Put a green card and a red card (or sticky notes) for each person on every table. Tell helpers: watch for red.
  2. Check the teacher page: it must say [number of students + 2] students at once (your own chat uses one seat, plus one spare). Too small? Ask Marcus before class.
  3. One person in two tabs, or on a laptop and a phone, uses two seats for up to 10 minutes. Use one tab only.
  4. Class 1 has no teacher prompts. Students type "Hello" in step 1.6.
  5. Open 3 tabs on the projector laptop: this class page, the teacher page, and github.com (signed in to your demo GitHub account).
  6. Have a sample resume file with fake details (fake street address and phone). You delete them on the screen in step 3.4. Never show your real private details.
  7. Have one real job posting open in a tab for checkpoint 4.
  8. Write the 6-character code on the whiteboard too. Then the code stays in view when you switch to your demo.
