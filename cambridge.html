  • Street address
  • Phone number

  • Other people's names, phones and emails

  • Your jobs, dates and schools
  • Your skills and numbers

  1. Teacher shows: the GitHub tab. Clicks + (top right) → New repository.

    You do: the same.

    You do: the same.

    Check: your resume text is in the big box.

    You do: in the big box, delete your street address, phone number and ID numbers. Read it one more time.

    You do: the same.

    Check: my-resume.txt is in your career-hub file list.

Why before Commit? GitHub keeps every old version of a file. If you delete your phone number later, the old version still has it.
Pasted from a PDF? Lines can break in strange places, and some letters can look wrong. That is OK. Fix only wrong words. Do not fix the layout. Nothing pastes at all? The PDF is a picture. Type your jobs, dates and skills, or ask a helper.
Why GitHub? Shared computers delete your Downloads, and the class chat does not keep your chat. GitHub keeps your work safe for the next class.
