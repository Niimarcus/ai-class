How the class chat works

1Join the class

On your laptop, open app.sparecycle.org/join and type the 6-character code from the screen. Click Continue. You can also scan the QR code, but then the chat opens on your phone.

[Screenshot TBD: Class link screen. Drop the image here once SYS-502 is on staging.]

2Read the privacy screen

Read the privacy text. Do not type names, addresses, A-numbers, case details, SSN or passport numbers. Sparecycle does not save what you type. On a shared or library computer, tick "I am on a shared or library computer". Click "I understand. Continue."

[Screenshot TBD: Language choice and privacy screen. Drop the image here once SYS-502 is on staging.]

3Tap a prompt, or type

Choose your answer language next to the box: English, Français or Kreyòl ayisyen (beta). Tap a button under "Prompts from your teacher". It puts the prompt in the box. It does not send. Write your words in each [ ] part. "Add a file" puts your resume text in the box. Delete private details. For French or Kreyòl, use the accent row for è ò à é ç. Press Send.

[Screenshot TBD: Chat, typing. Drop the image here once SYS-502 is on staging.]

4Read, listen, save

The answer appears. Press Listen to hear it. Change the text size if you want. A web page? Click "Save to project". Then open "Your files": "Preview" shows the page, "Download" saves the file. Save your work on GitHub.

[Screenshot TBD: Chat, answer. Drop the image here once SYS-502 is on staging.]

5If the chat stops

A message tells you what happened and what to do. Copy your work first. Then ask your teacher. Nothing you typed is saved. Before you go: "Your files", then "Download". Then "End class".
