Use the class chat in 6 steps

Scan the QR code on the projector, or type the link your teacher shows.

1Open the class link

Scan the QR code on the projector with your phone. Or type the link your teacher shows on your laptop.

[Screenshot TBD: Class link screen. Drop the image here once SYS-502 is on staging.]

2Read the privacy screen

Do not type names, addresses, A-numbers, case details, SSN or passport numbers. Sparecycle does not save what you type. Press the button to continue.

[Screenshot TBD: Privacy screen. Drop the image here once SYS-502 is on staging.]

3Pick your language

Choose Simple English, Kreyòl ayisyen or Français. Press Listen to hear the screen read aloud.

[Screenshot TBD: Language choice. Drop the image here once SYS-502 is on staging.]

4Type your question

Type in the box at the bottom. Use the accent row for è ò à é ç. Press Send.

[Screenshot TBD: Chat, typing. Drop the image here once SYS-502 is on staging.]

5Read, listen, save

The answer appears. Press Listen to hear it. Use A- or A+ to change the text size. Copy or save what you want to keep.

[Screenshot TBD: Chat, answer. Drop the image here once SYS-502 is on staging.]

6If the chat stops

A message tells you what happened and what to do. Copy your work first. Then ask your teacher. Nothing you typed is saved.
