How the class chat works

1Join the class

On your laptop, open app.sparecycle.org/join and type the 6-character code from the screen. Click Continue. You can also scan the QR code, but then the chat opens on your phone.

2Read the privacy screen

Read the privacy text. Do not type names, addresses, A-numbers, case details, SSN or passport numbers. Sparecycle does not save what you type. On a shared or library computer, tick "I am on a shared or library computer". Click "I understand. Continue."

3Tap a prompt, or type

Click "Answers" above the message box. Pick your answer language, for example Français (French) or Kreyòl ayisyen (Haitian Creole). Click ⋯ under the message box and pick a prompt from your teacher. It puts the prompt in the box. It does not send. Write your words in each [ ] part. "+" adds a file: your resume text goes in the box. Delete private details. For French or Kreyòl, use the accent row for è ò à é ç. Press Send.

4Read, listen, save

The answer appears. Press Listen to hear it. Change the text size if you want. A web page builds live on the right. Click "Save to project" on its code box. Then open "Files": "Download" saves the file, "Open in GitHub" puts it in your repo.

5If the chat stops

A message tells you what happened and what to do. Copy your work first. Then ask your teacher. Nothing you typed is saved. Before you go: "Files", then "Download". Then "End class".
