1My live link shows "404"

What you see: A white page that says 404: There isn't a GitHub Pages site here.

What to do:

  1. Wait 2 minutes. Then refresh the page.
  2. Check the link: your-username.github.io/portfolio/. Spelling matters.
  3. Open the repo. The file must be named exactly index.html.
  4. Not Index.html, not index.html.txt, not inside a folder.
  5. Check the repo says Public next to its name.
  6. Go to Settings → Pages. Branch must be main. Click Save.

2My photo does not show

What you see: Your initials show in a circle, not your photo.

What to do:

  1. The photo must be in the portfolio repo, next to index.html.
  2. The name must be photo.jpg, photo.png or photo.jpeg. All small letters.
  3. Windows trap: you typed photo.jpg, but the file is photo.jpg.png.
  4. Fix: rename the file to just photo. Upload it again.
  5. iPhone photo may be HEIC. Upload a screenshot of it instead.
  6. Wait 1 minute. Press Ctrl+Shift+R to refresh.
  7. Still no photo? Your index.html may be old. Ask a helper.

3Wrong repo, or my repo is private

What you see: You uploaded files, but nothing changes on your live link.

What to do:

  1. Look at the repo name at the top of GitHub.
  2. career-hub is private. It never makes a website. That is on purpose.
  3. Your website files go in portfolio. It must be Public.
  4. To move a file: open it, click Copy raw file.
  5. In portfolio: Add file → Create new file. Same name. Paste. Commit.
  6. Never make career-hub public. It has your private notes.

4Claude says I reached the limit

What you see: A message like You are out of free messages until 5:00 PM.

What to do:

  1. Do not worry. Your work is saved in GitHub.
  2. Write down the time Claude shows.
  3. Do the steps with no AI: saving, checklists, practice.
  4. Sit with a partner. Help them and watch.
  5. After that time, start a New chat. Paste the prompt again.

5The Copy button did nothing

What you see: You click Copy, but paste gives old text or nothing.

What to do:

  1. See yellow text Press Ctrl+C to copy? The text is selected.
  2. Press Ctrl+C (on a Mac: Cmd+C). Then paste.
  3. No yellow text? Click the start, then Shift+click the end.
  4. On a phone: press and hold the text, then tap Copy.

6I lost my chat

What you see: Claude shows an empty chat. Your old chat is gone from the screen.

What to do:

  1. In Claude, open the left side menu. Click Chats or Recents.
  2. Search for a word from your chat, like your name.
  3. Cannot find it? That is OK. Your files are in GitHub.
  4. Start a New chat. Paste the prompt again with your saved text.

7My page looks broken after I paste

What you see: The page shows code as text, is only half there, or has no colors.

What to do:

  1. Open the file on GitHub. The first line must be <!DOCTYPE html>.
  2. The last line must be </html>.
  3. Delete any Claude words before the first line or after the last.
  4. No </html> at the end? Claude's answer was cut off.
  5. Tell Claude: "You stopped early. Give me the complete file again."
  6. Check the file name ends in .html, not .txt.
  7. Click in the edit box. Press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+V.

8I forgot my password, or the sign-in code

What you see: You cannot sign in to Claude or GitHub.

What to do:

  1. Claude has no password. It emails you a code or link.
  2. Open your email first. Or use Continue with Google.
  3. GitHub: click Forgot password?. Open the email link. Make a new password.
  4. GitHub asks for a 6-number code (2FA)? Open your phone's code app.
  5. Lost your phone? Click Use a recovery code. Type a saved code.
  6. No recovery codes? Tell a helper. Work with a partner today.
Still stuck after the steps? Write down what you see (or take a photo of the screen). Show it to a helper.
