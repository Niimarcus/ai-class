Set up your GitHub account before class (15 minutes)

Do this by Tuesday, October 27. Class 1 is Wednesday, October 28, 6:00 to 8:00 pm, Cambridge Public Library.

4 steps

1Go to github.com/signup

Use a computer if you can. Type your email, a password and a username. Use your name for the username, like amina-diallo. Write the password in your notebook.

[Screenshot TBD: GitHub sign-up page]

2Do the check, then get your code

GitHub shows a small puzzle. Solve it. Then open your email. Find the message from GitHub. Type the code, or click the link, as the email says.

[Screenshot TBD: Email from GitHub]

3Check it worked

You see your GitHub home page. Click your picture (top right). You see your username. Done.

4Sign out

Click your picture, then Sign out. Do this on a shared computer.

Bring to class 1

  • Your laptop and charger, or come at 5:30 pm for help

  • Your resume in any form (Word, Google Docs, PDF, or half a page you write)
  • One job posting you want: the link
