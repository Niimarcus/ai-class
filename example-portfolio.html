About Me (&)
My Journey

Good intentions fail without good logistics — eight years of health programs taught me that, on two continents.

'16

First 14,000 records

Ran Korle Bu Teaching Hospital's patient-records digitization pilot and scheduled 40+ clinical staff across 6 wards — rota conflicts cut 60%.

'19

Three regions, one goal

Took over stalling maternal-health outreach at HealthReach Ghana and rebuilt it around market-day clinics and 60 trained community volunteers.

'21

Zero downtime

Moved 12 rural clinics onto the national EHR: pilot one, write the playbook, train local champions, scale. Not one unplanned downtime day.

'22

The cold chain held

45,000 vaccine doses through a fragile cold chain at 98% compliance — on simple checklists nurses actually used.

'25

Boston, new chapter

Family and ambition brought me to the U.S. Crossing systems and cultures is exactly the muscle complex care teams need.

'26

Built this site

Designed my AI-powered career hub with Claude and GitHub Pages — five weeks in, no code background.
